Undergraduate · 2019–2020

Led exercise sessions and evaluated homework for this first-year course in mathematical methods for economists. I emphasized step-by-step problem solving and the economic applications of calculus, Taylor approximations, and constrained optimization.

Undergraduate · 2020

Stockholm University

Microeconomics I

PhD · 2022

Macroeconomics II

PhD · 2023
